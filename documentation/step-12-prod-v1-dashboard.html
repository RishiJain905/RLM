Overview

Where the run stands

rlm/cost

Inference spend

Every rollout, sub-call, KL re-score and eval is paid from the W&B inference pot. These are the loop's estimates at the research's prices; W&B's billing page is the truth.

rlm/pass · dynamic sampling

Pass rates and difficulty

Each step draws tasks at the controller's levels and runs a group of rollouts of each. A family moves up a level above a 70% pass rate over its last 3 steps and down below 30%, or at once when at least 8 of its episodes in a row were all right or all wrong.

rlm/policy · actor

Policy health

Entropy and within-group reward std are the collapse signals behind the learning-rate rule. Grey lines are the thresholds the loop checks.

rlm/shape

RLM behaviour

How the root model uses the REPL, over every episode: sub-calls against the budget, turns against the cap, and how long its transcripts get. Prompt v4 (from prod-v1's checkpoint 20) allows 80 sub-calls an episode, each reading up to 60,000 characters and writing up to 4,096 tokens, 20 turns for read episodes and 30 for edit episodes, and 4,096 output tokens a root turn. Before it: 30 sub-calls, 12 and 30 turns, and 3,072 tokens a read turn. Edit episodes run 300 s cells, read episodes 120 s.

rlm/edit · spec §12.9

Editing

Edit episodes (edit spec, build from a spec, and edit bugs, find and fix, since prod-v1's checkpoint 20; edit real and edit synth before it) change a repository, and hidden tests grade the diff: the reward is 1 only when every fail-to-pass and pass-to-pass test passes. They run with 30 turns (20 before prompt v3), 80 sub-calls (30 before prompt v4), 4,096 tokens a turn and 300 s cells, and the REPL gives them seven coding tools: read_file, find_files, grep, edit_file, write_file, shell and diff. None of the behaviour below is rewarded; it shows whether the gains come the RLM way.

rlm/reward

Reward and cheating

Exact accuracy is the read families' headline, and resolved (every hidden test passing) the edit families'. A firing hack detector zeroes the rollout's reward before the group mean, so a hack becomes a negative example. Network try is the one rule that is only logged.

rlm/infra · rlm/time

Infra and timing

Infra episodes never reach training; for edit episodes that includes a grader that couldn't start. Above 1% the run pauses for Rishi.
