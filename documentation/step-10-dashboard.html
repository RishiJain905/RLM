Overview

Where the run stands

rlm/cost

Inference spend

Every rollout, sub-call, KL re-score and eval is paid from the W&B inference pot. These are the loop's estimates at the research's prices; W&B's billing page is the truth.

rlm/pass · dynamic sampling

Pass rates and difficulty

Each step draws 8 tasks at the controller's levels and runs 4 rollouts of each. A family moves up a level above a 70% pass rate over its last 3 steps and down below 20%.

rlm/policy · actor

Policy health

Entropy and within-group reward std are the collapse signals behind the learning-rate rule. Grey lines are the thresholds the loop checks.

rlm/shape

RLM behaviour

How the root model uses the REPL, over every episode: sub-calls against the budget of 30, turns against the cap (12 for read episodes, 20 for edit episodes), and how long its transcripts get. Edit episodes also get 4,096 output tokens a turn and 300 s cells, where read episodes keep 3,072 and 120 s.

rlm/edit · spec §12.9

Editing

Edit episodes (edit real and edit synth) change a repository, and hidden tests grade the diff: the reward is 1 only when every fail-to-pass and pass-to-pass test passes. They run with 20 turns, 30 sub-calls, 4,096 tokens a turn and 300 s cells, and the REPL gives them seven coding tools: read_file, find_files, grep, edit_file, write_file, shell and diff. None of the behaviour below is rewarded; it shows whether the gains come the RLM way.

rlm/reward

Reward and cheating

Exact accuracy is the read families' headline, and resolved (every hidden test passing) the edit families'. A firing hack detector zeroes the rollout's reward before the group mean, so a hack becomes a negative example. Network try is the one rule that is only logged.

rlm/infra · rlm/time

Infra and timing

Infra episodes never reach training; for edit episodes that includes a grader that couldn't start. Above 1% the run pauses for Rishi.
